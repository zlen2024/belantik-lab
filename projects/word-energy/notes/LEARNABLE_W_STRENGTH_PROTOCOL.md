# Matched total W strength: frozen training protocol

22 November 2026. Hypothesis: learning total interaction strength can improve context discrimination relative to the same architecture with strength fixed. This first stage trains matched checkpoints; it cannot by itself answer the sense question. Natural-language sense evaluation and lexical evaluation must be separately frozen before examining their scores. Use the already prepared WikiText source-fit dataset `3387fc4fb99646f1b7aacd518f39bf10`; only its fit arrays may be read during training. The previously used WikiText development and SemCor inner-development are not blind; their outer reserves stay closed.

Use six spins, exact classical pair partition by butterfly contraction, three seeds 17, 29, 43; 10,000 ordered pairs, five provided noise draws, three epochs, 128 minibatch, Adam .01 with global-norm clipping 5, same prior and objective as `train_spin8_prospective.py`. Initialize lexical unary parameters identically per seed. In both arms use `g_i = λ softmax(r)_i`, `r=0`, initial `λ=0.8`: fixed arm sets λ=.8; learned arm uses `λ=1.5 sigmoid(a)`, `a=log(.8/.7)`. Shape r, unary parameters, gradient calculation and all updates are otherwise identical; fixed arm freezes a's update. Global clipping is computed after masking fixed a gradient. Record full, a-only and relative-gradient norms per epoch, λ and g trajectory, train loss, checkpoint and data hashes. Bounds: λ in (0,1.5), all g positive. CPU only; at most 900 s and 2 GiB peak per run.

Before each seed's training, assert identical initial params, scores and non-a gradients, confirm finite a gradient and finite-difference derivative on real fit batch. Both arms must receive exactly the same fit order/noise. Record six separate runs; no selection based on their training losses. This isolates ability to adjust a shared positive diagonal coupling sum within this pair-training setup; it does not test signed/dense W, spin count, learning a physical spring, multiword contextual training, Gaussian, THRML sampling or chip operation. Later matched sense readout should normalize 2–4 spokes by cue count and compare λ=0, fixed, learned, frequency and context baselines using only fit-derived sense maps and already defined inner split.
