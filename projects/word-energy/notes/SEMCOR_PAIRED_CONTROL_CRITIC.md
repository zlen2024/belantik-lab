# Independent critique: paired SemCor controls

GPT-6.2 Sol, medium, independently reconstructed the 2,000-replicate document bootstrap from the logged paired rows and exactly reproduced its interval. Every target/sense count and correctness total matched the earlier grouped run; the logged script matched the frozen source and run status was FINISHED. No outer-reserved XML was read.

Among the 31 scored document clusters, 18 favored cues, nine favored majority and four tied. Omitting one document at a time gave pooled improvements of +1.56 to +2.41 percentage points; the wide document-bootstrap interval reflects joint sampling variability rather than one obviously decisive document. This is descriptive sensitivity, not a substitute for an independent evaluation or model comparison.

Shuffle limitations: 107/1,279 = 8.37% of rows are singleton target/cue-count groups and cannot move; other shuffled bags may remain fixed or identical. Bags may cross documents, so the shuffle is not a document-preserving significance test. The program uses one seeded RNG stream, consuming bootstrap draws before its 200 permutation draws; the protocol's phrase independently seeded is imprecise. Reproduction must follow that order. It records only shuffle summary quantiles, not all individual permutations. Coverage of the selected two-sense subset is 1,279/1,735 (73.72%) of eligible appearances of selected words. This run does not test W, either energy model, or hardware.

The result is consistent with a modest supervised lexical-context signal, but its document-bootstrap interval includes zero and does not establish durable gains. The next research step should prioritize a matched fixed-versus-learned coupling experiment rather than continuing to treat benchmark plumbing as model progress. Match graph, `g_i=λr_i`, initialization, seeds, training budgets, cue-count normalization and a fit-only readout; compare zero-W and context-only controls, and separately establish the Gaussian comparator. A physical chip claim requires a real hardware run.
