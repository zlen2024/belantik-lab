For each checkpoint calculate exact six-spin unary probabilities over 64 states and exact shared-center posterior with 2–4 cue leaves, per-spoke `g/k`, inverse temperature 1. For each target and selected sense, form the mean target posterior over its inner-fit examples at the checkpoint's native λ (.8 fixed, learned λ learned). These supervised 64-dimensional centroids are each checkpoint's fixed sense map. Score every selected validation example at λ=0, λ=.8 and the same-seed learned final λ, retaining that checkpoint's normalized relative g distribution and centroids. Predict the lexicographically first sense on exact distance ties, otherwise smallest squared Euclidean distance to centroid. No validation labels choose centroids, λ or targets. Each checkpoint's native centroids differ, so crossover tests inference strength with a fixed map, not reoptimized readout. Confirm exact λ=0 posterior equals unary and is cue independent.

Primary descriptive metric: mean per-target balanced accuracy for the fixed 94 targets containing both selected senses in validation, separately by seed, arm, inference λ. Report all conditions, target and occurrence counts, cue count strata, and majority plus the previously frozen supervised Naive Bayes context baseline.
