## Models and common objective

Three original training arms plus one shuffled-joint control, seeds17/29/43, 12 runs. Tied Ising token has two spins (3V+2 =6002 parameters); all three occurrences are distinct spin copies even when IDs repeat. q_w(s) is normalized unary probability for 4 states; g=1.5 sigmoid(raw interaction). Candidate center w and contexts a,b use:

`S_joint = log sum_s q_w(s) [sum_t q_a(t) exp((g/2)·(s*t))] [sum_t q_b(t) exp((g/2)·(s*t))] + 0.25 log count(w)`.

This is exact normalized six-spin star log partition, 7 edges and maximum degree3 (two center internal/leaf internal and four spokes); beta=1. `pair_add` sums two independently normalized F2(w,a) and F2(w,b), each with g/2 per spoke, and adds the same prior once; identical tied Ising parameters and initial values. Matched 3D **shared** vector uses dot(v_w, (v_a+v_b)/2) and same prior, 3V=6000 parameters. Same train sample/center noises/optimizer/budget, loss `mean softplus(1−S_positive+S_noise)`, Adam lr .01, clip5, batch256, 3 epochs. Fixed epoch3; no test or data-dependent tuning. Joint-vs-additive tests joint energy computation under this particular training procedure, not evidence all pairwise models fail. Count-matching is not expressivity-matching.
