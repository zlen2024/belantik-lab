# SemCor train-only sense-anchor feasibility

Freeze before reading sense labels beyond an illustrative format sample. Source is the local NLTK-distributed SemCor 3.0 archive `semcor.zip`, SHA-256 `126fa2e829ab63edd5b3fd9de45ef1d60d6880e01e25abc55b5ac7918a824655`. Its README says SemCor 3.0 automatically maps SemCor 1.6 annotations to WordNet 3.0 and marks missing mappings with `wnsn=0`. Source labels are human sense annotations plus automatic version mapping, not gold for every occurrence. Retain archive and its license locally; log hash and no raw text to Git.

Only list XML document paths initially. Split 352 unique base document names by SHA-256 of `semcor-fit-v1:<basename>` sorted: first 282 are anchor-fit, remaining 70 reserved. Read **only anchor-fit XML** in this feasibility audit. Never read the remaining 70 labels or official WSD test data. Use the vocabulary from already-trained `3387fc4fb99646f1b7aacd518f39bf10` (2,000 words derived from separate WikiText fit documents); verify vocabulary hash. This source and filtering constrain generality; other pretrained data overlap must be audited separately.

Each single-token target must be alphabetic, lowercase surface equal to its `lemma`, present in vocabulary, `cmd=done`, POS beginning NN (nouns only), single unambiguous `lexsn`, and `wnsn` nonzero and not list-valued. WordNet sense identity is `(lemma,lexsn)`; verify format and exclude multiple annotations instead of guessing. For context take sentence elements within ±8 positions, retain alphabetic vocabulary tokens excluding the first 30 vocabulary words by fit-only frequency, order by distance then left before right, and keep up to four occurrences; require at least two. This is an algorithmic content-word heuristic, not proof of semantic sufficiency. Keep document/sentence IDs and sense keys, not raw text, in local artifacts.

For each target, count eligible occurrences and unique documents per sense. A candidate anchor pair requires at least five training occurrences in at least three training documents for **each** of two different sense keys. If >2 senses qualify, choose the top two by training count, breaking ties lexically by sense key. Report candidate target count, per-sense occurrence/document coverage, examples, target names and POS, and exclusion counts; no heldout information may choose targets or sense maps. Candidate selection uses train labels only. No scoring/training or Gaussian/THRML/chip inference in this run. Human annotation and sense frequency do not guarantee two contexts have visually sufficient cues, and no text-overlap with WikiText or other sources is ruled out. Preserve hashes, reproducible manifest and source snapshots in MLflow.
