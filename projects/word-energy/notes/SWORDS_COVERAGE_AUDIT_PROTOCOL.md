# Label-blind SWORDS development coverage audit

Freeze before computing coverage. Source the official SWORDS v1.1 development dataset at `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/p-lambda/swords/main/assets/parsed/swords-v1.1_dev.json.gz`, SHA-256 `bc3570786455a05f11fcb4fc7f54e117642d5fb5e98f19355028c5c7ee00c954`. The official [benchmark repository](https://github.com/p-lambda/swords) describes the development data, its CC-BY-3.0-US license and the dataset schema. [Lee et al. 2021](https://aclanthology.org/2021.naacl-main.345/) describe context-dependent lexical substitution and candidate judgments. Keep downloaded content local with attribution and source/hash; do not fetch or inspect the test split.

Only inspect unlabeled development input fields and candidate strings. Do not read, count, filter, or evaluate judgment labels, and do not score models. Measure coverage against the frozen 2,000-token WikiText training vocabulary: target token in-vocabulary; exact single ASCII-letter token/span; nonempty in-vocabulary context within ±2 tokens using the existing WiC tokenizer; candidate substitute is a single alphabetic token, is different from the target, and is in vocabulary. Report target totals, context coverage, candidate counts and the number with at least two distinct candidate IDs. Compute a conservative exact long-context match screen against normalized WikiText train and validation paragraphs to flag potential overlap; failure to detect exact text does not prove independent sources or no partial overlap. Describe coverage loss at each stage and do not inspect candidate quality labels to select a subset or model.

Save only counts, data hashes, coverage rules and source snapshot in MLflow. Avoid raw benchmark text in Git; raw data is local under `data/swords`. This audit is for feasibility of a later separately specified, label-safe benchmark; eligibility with at least two candidates alone does not ensure distinguishable positive and negative judged substitutes. An actual meaningful contextual comparison needs a frozen scorer consistent with original Q/K/window semantics, a matched vector baseline, a justified Gaussian implementation and coverage, candidate-label interpretation, source-aware uncertainty and a clean independent test protocol. Do not call the unlabeled coverage audit a model result or proof of generalization, polysemy or chip capability.
