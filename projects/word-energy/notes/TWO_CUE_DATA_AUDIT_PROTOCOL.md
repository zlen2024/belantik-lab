# Two-cue data and supervision audit

Freeze before running. This audit checks the proposed synthetic generator's information structure before any model fitting. It does not demonstrate that a word-energy embedding learns meaning or compare models.

Use four opaque cue IDs for each of A0, A1, B0, B1. Every A/B family pair has 16 cue-ID combinations. Partition by `(index_a+index_b) mod 4`: residues 0/1 train and residue 2 development. Compute aggregate counts for residue 3 for disjointness and coverage only; do not form reserved model inputs, predictions or evaluate a model on them. Create balanced training positives for each train pair: ambiguous center T and anchor S_y, where y is XOR of the latent cue-family values. Mark center substitution S_(1-y) as an oracle negative for the anchor window. Derive pair and center counts, support sets and deterministic split manifests. Labels and oracle relationships are generator supervision, never claim natural text or unsupervised discovery.

Audit four questions: (1) every cue ID occurs in train and development and no pair crosses splits; every split is balanced per cell and each single cue predicts y at chance; (2) the balanced T inputs occur identically whether their unobserved semantic label is y or its complement, whereas the anchor windows' center identity differs, so parity labels for T do not follow by definition from T cooccurrences alone; (3) build the original six ordered lexical pairs from anchor-positive and T-positive windows and enumerate all cue/anchor pairs to determine if any proposed cross-family pair is actually invalid; and (4) demonstrate that a proposed T-to-D corruption, with D never appearing as a positive center, is perfectly detected by center identity alone. This is an audit of that proposed shortcut, not a claim that all possible corruption designs fail.

No model training, validation threshold tuning, random corpus search, or reserved-test model evaluation. Print exact counts and assertions, run under deterministic Python, and log the script, this protocol, actual manifest hash, counts, and limitations to local MLflow. Seek an independent critic to assess the mathematical argument, any defective code, original-plan alignment, and one evidence-grounded next step. The reserved manifest is a deterministic combinatorial design, not an independent linguistic benchmark.
