# Fixed and trainable relative W control

22 November 2026. Posthoc follow-up after the `c61cdc3` pilot and its critic observed that replacing final couplings at inference with their initial value improved all six Ising validation scores. This observation selected the intervention, so the reused validation data are exploratory and not independent confirmation.

Reuse exactly the same 10,000 training and 2,000 validation windows, offsets, negatives, orders, seeds 17/29/43, 3 epochs, batch 128, optimizer, architecture and final-epoch readout. Two new conditions for each d=4 and d=6: (1) `fixed`, all cross couplings fixed at `g_i=.8/d`, no cross-W gradient; (2) `redistribute`, W remains trainable and `g_i=(.8+.3*(tanh(u_i)-mean_j tanh(u_j)))/d`. Its sum is always `.8`, and `0.2/d < g_i < 1.4/d < 1.5/d`; signed variations between coordinates remain possible even though every actual coupling stays positive. Identical raw initialization, all values begin at `.8/d`. Unary biases and internal couplings remain trainable in both conditions. The fixed condition is an attribution control, not a proposal to discard learnable W. W is diagonal, shared and bounded; this is not arbitrary pairwise W or a physical spring constant.
