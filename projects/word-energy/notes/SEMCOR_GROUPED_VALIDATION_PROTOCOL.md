# SemCor grouped development feasibility: frozen protocol

This is a bounded development-only test of whether a sense benchmark can discriminate simple baselines. It does not train or evaluate an Ising or Gaussian model. The 70 outer reserved documents stay unopened, and no official WSD test is used. Reuse the archive and vocabulary hashes from `SEMCOR_TRAIN_ANCHOR_FEASIBILITY_PROTOCOL.md` and the same 282 outer-fit filenames. Split those filenames by SHA-256 of `semcor-inner-v1:<basename>`, ordered: first 225 inner-fit, last 57 inner-validation. Determine both splits before opening XML. Parse only these 282 files.

Apply the previous surface/lemma, vocabulary, label and ±8 position filters, but explicitly require POS `NN`, remove occurrences of the target word from cues, and require at least two distinct retained cue IDs among the first four eligible cues. Selection and sense ranking use inner-fit labels only: two sense keys with at least five occurrences and three documents each, sorted by decreasing frequency then key. Count, but exclude, inner-validation occurrences of any other sense; report this coverage limitation. Report numbers of targets and validation examples, selected and excluded senses and words with both selected senses represented. Include per-target counts.

Freeze two supervised probes: (1) predict the most frequent selected sense per target in inner-fit, lexicographic sense key for ties; (2) multinomial naive Bayes on the same up-to-four cue IDs, per-target priors and Laplace α=1 over the entire fixed 2000-word vocabulary; lexicographic tie rule. The same two selected labels and examples apply to both. Report overall accuracy, target-macro accuracy, and target-macro balanced accuracy only among validation targets with both selected senses present (with target count); also report majority and cue predictions' paired differences. These are development baselines, not word embedding or generalization claims. Source selection is retrospective since outer-fit was already audited; inner split reduces label leakage but is not blind preregistration. Identical sentences and cross-corpus overlap remain possible. Deterministic CPU only; no tuning or held-out inspection.
