This repeated-development diagnostic tests an original-plan mechanism without retraining: does observed context change the target's distribution across candidate-derived regions, and does that change track the existing weak preferences? For each seed, word unary q is computed from that frozen checkpoint. Define a region of the 16 states by `q_A(s)>q_B(s)`; reverse for B and assign half credit to both for exact ties. These regions depend only on the two candidate unaries, never context or weak preference, and are consistent for every occurrence of the same candidate pair within that checkpoint. They are **candidate-derived probes**, not independently validated word-sense regions; candidates are provided by annotations, not generated by the model. Since the unaries are learned, a preference-region relation may reflect lexical frequency or other shortcuts.
