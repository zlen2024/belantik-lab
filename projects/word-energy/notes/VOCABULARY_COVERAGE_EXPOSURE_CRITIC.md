# Critic: vocabulary coverage and training exposure

21 November 2026. GPT-6.1 Sol ultra independently rebuilt the train-only frequency vocabularies and label-blind SWORDS coverage, and reproduced all counts and hashes in MLflow `b255997743f34449bebac134a19496ea`. It separately reimplemented pair/window sampling, reproducing all candidate arrays and hashes in `32daa45ead0441a2bb1d1d8ac6365fad`, and checked original data and source snapshots. Neither audit evaluated a model or SWORDS judgment labels.

The input coverage improvements are mechanical, not evidence of semantic learning. Across all 370 SWORDS development targets, 22,967 target-specific candidate identities remain after the audit's surface/lemma exclusions; 18,707 are single ASCII tokens. The 2k, 5k, 10k and 19,871 vocabularies include 4,049, 7,025, 9,795 and 12,378 of the 22,967 respectively. These include candidate identities outside the finally eligible target subset and do not count accepted candidates. The 2k and 10k cutoffs have raw training frequencies 100 and 13 respectively. The full count≥5 vocabulary contains 7,355 types seen only five to nine times in the corpus.

The candidate sample differs materially from the original. Exactly 3,753 distinct raw occurrence windows occur in both the original 141,101 unique windows and the prospective 158,777 unique windows, after keeping the center fixed and sorting the two context positions. No corresponding row is identical. Of 200,000 new windows, 92,456 contain at least one word outside the original 2k. A performance change after retraining cannot be attributed to vocabulary size alone. A common-support evaluation and a separate expanded-coverage evaluation must both be reported, with sampling and compute differences stated.

In ranks 5,001–10,000, 977 words have zero sampled positive center occurrences and 861 have one. Median is two sampled center appearances but one distinct raw center position, maximum eight distinct center positions. Forty words never occur anywhere in the 200,000 windows. Ranks 2,001–5,000 have 45 zero centers and medians seven sampled / four unique raw center positions. The old common 2k center count falls from 200,000 to 163,114 at the same sample budget. Three presentations of each sample do not increase the number of underlying distinct contexts. Full softmax still gives negative-class gradients to words with no positive occurrence, so these figures do not imply no parameter updates.

The 10k full-softmax arithmetic evaluates six billion candidate scores across 600k window presentations, versus 1.2 billion for 2k. This is not a measured latency or energy ratio. A larger host vocabulary does not by itself increase the active per-window spin graph. The original Ising plan still requires useful contextual behavior and sampled same-system observable parity; the Gaussian comparator and physical-hardware evidence remain separate.

Critic used pinned Lean 4.36.0 and Mathlib `fc4f59e577feb1a4956671bc04ac18aaaf8cd842`, via `lake env lean --stdin`, to prove for finite sets `v ⊆ w → card(c ∩ v) ≤ card(c ∩ w)` and preservation of any candidate-count threshold. `#print axioms` reported `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`, without `sorryAx`. This formalizes only monotonic coverage under nested vocabularies, not semantic improvement, corpus independence or Python implementation.

The current evidence does not identify 10k as an optimum or demonstrate that scaling will improve contextual sense. A next development proposal should specify task-aligned learning and the original same-window readout, match Ising and vector data, report fixed common-support and expanded-coverage panels, explicitly measure training budget/cost, and separately audit the exact same observable against THRML. Keep the official test closed. A vocabulary sweep or a simple scaling result alone would not establish replacement readiness.
