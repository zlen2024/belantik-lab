# Independent critique: SemCor train-only feasibility

Review by GPT-6.2 Sol, medium effort, 22 November 2026. The reviewer independently parsed the 282 fit documents and reconstructed the recorded artifacts, including all 201 target pairs, 402 sense keys and 8,135 selected occurrences. The archive and vocabulary hashes, split manifests and eligible count of 18,643 matched. Reserved XML documents were not opened. All eligible targets observed were noun POS `NN`, with noun-type sense keys; case folding introduced no additional rows, and within the fit data `(lemma, wnsn)` and `lexsn` were mutually consistent.

Important limitations: 71 targets have more than two senses satisfying the thresholds, and 165 have eligible occurrences from nonselected senses (2,099 occurrences). Thus the proposed two-sense task must handle other senses explicitly, rather than treating them as either selected sense. One selected sense has only three fit documents for 16 targets, and at most five for 94; 31 selected lower-frequency senses have only five occurrences. 193 selected inputs contain another occurrence of the target as a cue, and 15 have only one distinct cue even though the rule requires two cue occurrences. These conditions can confound a future semantic task; predefine filters and report coverage before evaluation.

No exact duplicate fit occurrences or duplicate normalized sentences of at least ten words across fit documents were found; this does not establish absence of leakage across corpora or reserved documents. The protocol's phrase `already-trained 3387...` is inaccurate: `3387fc4fb99646f1b7aacd518f39bf10` is the dataset/vocabulary run, not a trained model checkpoint. The audit uses only its recorded vocabulary.

To isolate the user's W hypothesis, compare identical coupling parameterizations `g_i = λ r_i` and matching initialization, data and compute, with fixed versus learned total `λ`; measure learned λ, coupling distribution, gradients and saturation. Include zero-interaction and context controls and frequency/context-only baselines. First freeze and evaluate a document-grouped validation within fit data for the availability and behavior of the sense task; keep the 70 reserved documents sealed. A learned Ising interaction coefficient alone is not evidence of physical spring stiffness or execution on hardware.
