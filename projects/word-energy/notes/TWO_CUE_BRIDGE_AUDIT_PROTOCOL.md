# Candidate bridge data audit

This prespecified deterministic audit examines whether a *candidate* four-token extension supplies target-specific information absent from the earlier two-cue draft. It does not train a model or claim that artificial XOR is natural language. Original plans allow a larger active window, but the existing three-token implementation and sampler must be adapted and audited separately.

Use exactly the same cue-pair split as `TWO_CUE_DATA_AUDIT_PROTOCOL.md` and two targets T0 and T1. For each cue pair and target type t, assign anchor center S_y, where y=a XOR b XOR t. Training positive window is `(S_y,T_t,A_a,B_b)`; the balanced oracle corruption substitutes S_(1-y) while keeping the other three IDs unchanged. Both targets, all cues and both anchors are observed in training. Labels exist only in the generator and analysis; models see window IDs and positive/negative membership, with identical supervision for all proposed arms. Training anchor identity provides explicit synthetic supervision.

Compute exhaustive label and positive/negative support tables. Report information-theoretic empirical entropy of y overall and conditional on T, A, B, any pair and all three, and the same computed using actual opaque cue IDs rather than family values. Report center-only positive/negative classification accuracy and full-window positive/negative collisions. Verify train/dev/reserved cue-pair disjointness, all token coverage, equal cell counts and label balance. Reserved model inputs are not emitted or evaluated. Flag if the split or a cue ID leaks labels. Audit whether removing T or either cue makes the label unpredictable on both training and development pairs under this balanced construction. This is necessary information availability, not proof model objective gradients will encode target senses, that four-spin Ising can fit the task, or that the task generalizes to natural text.

Log code, protocol, deterministic train/development manifests, hashes and outputs to the local MLflow ledger. Afterward ask critic to inspect whether the proposed bridge is genuinely aligned to the two original plans, whether a simpler data signal or an implicit label leaks, and whether a fresh language-based direction should take priority. Do not run a four-token model until its scorer, graph, controls, training/evaluation protocol, costs and source-of-supervision limitations have been specified and independently checked.
