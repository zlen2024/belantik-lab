Four relevant existing tests passed according to the critic. The WikiText Ising-minus-vector MRR was -0.00009981, 95% interval [-0.00409974, 0.00374011]. ## Experiment and hypothesis

The critic found no protocol or implementation error warranting rerunning the matched comparison. Both models use the same data, orders, shared log-count offset, Adam/clipping, batch, epochs and candidate vocabulary, but matched updates and parameter counts are not matched compute. The vector training took 20.74/24.43/20.26 s versus Ising 49.89/48.52/48.59 s in separate local jobs. Runtime is contextual and not a hardware benchmark. WikiText recall@10 was approximately 0.35987 Ising, 0.36710 vector and 0.33810 prior. EWT recall@10 was 0.26473, 0.27950 and 0.23170. Both panels are reused development and the bootstrap conditions on the selected documents and seeds.

The result contradicts a prediction that this 4-spin configuration and objective would beat the specified tied 10D vector on full-vocabulary MRR. It does not disprove other landscape representations. The requested advantage of a context-sensitive multi-well word landscape has not been established. Original Ising plan requires a shared active-window system, credible posterior/correlation readout, exact-to-THRML parity and graph/precision audit. Its current full-vocabulary retrieval instead computes candidate-wise normalized partition scores and a host frequency offset, and previous THRML precision/rare-event gates failed. Gibbs samples do not by themselves supply partition functions across candidate windows. The Gaussian plan is a continuous reference; its D=1/M=2 toy accuracy hit the ceiling for lexical and vector controls, while the sampler audit addressed numerical feasibility. Neither plan justifies a replacement or physical-chip claim from retrieval alone.

## Direction

Keep the existing Ising and vector checkpoints as references. The critic's next bounded action is a frozen, balanced two-cue context contrast that checks whether changing one cue while holding the other fixed changes the supported sense. Audit per-sense floors, single-cue/frequency/identity shortcuts, cue combinations and vocabulary coverage. Include same-parameter additive and zero-interaction Ising readouts, a nonlinear contextual vector capable of interaction, and Gaussian joint versus frozen lexical controls where a common task is actually defined. A symmetric star and tied mean-context vector cannot distinguish swapping the same two context tokens, so order alone must not be the label. Ensure the shared-context hypotheses are falsifiable rather than creating a toy that only a chosen architecture can solve. After a genuine contextual signal, check its single-window observable against exact and THRML sampling with a separately frozen rare-event budget and graph/precision audit.

Avoid treating further MRR sweeps, more spins, repeated ceiling-prone synthetic tests or chip mapping of candidate retrieval as direct evidence for the missing contextual property. The critic did not choose an experiment using reserved test labels.
