GPT-6.2 Sol, medium, independently re-parsed the same 282 permitted XML files, verified source snapshot hashes and reproduced split manifests, all per-target counts and aggregate scores in MLflow run `bd4c107ad9654a1288761a207dcd7d98`. The 70 outer-reserved XML files remained closed. Two exact naive Bayes ties were identified; exact rational arithmetic agreed with the implemented log-score tie decisions.

The cue model and majority model were both correct on 793 examples and both wrong on 346; the cue model alone was correct on 83 and majority alone on 57. Only 119 of 225 inner-fit and 31 of 57 inner-validation files contributed eligible nouns. Of the 94 words with both senses in validation, 34 have just one validation example in at least one sense. Excluding 456 other-sense cases means coverage is 1,279 / 1,735 = 73.72% among eligible appearances of selected words. This is conditional two-sense classification, not all-sense evaluation.

The reviewer found no exact normalized whole-sentence overlap against fit eligible noun rows, or exact `(target, unordered cue bag)` repeats against selected-sense fit rows for scored validation examples. This limited check does not rule out near duplicates or cross-corpus overlap. No uncertainty interval was calculated; 31 contributing validation documents and sparse senses limit interpretation.

The result artifact omits details of ten train-selected targets without scored validation rows, per-target excluded-sense counts, and row-level paired predictions/document IDs. The 1,279 scored examples have 2, 3, and 4 cues in 228, 287, and 764 cases respectively; a W experiment should specify spoke normalization and examine cue-count strata. Naive Bayes uses supervised target-specific counts, and its improvement could reflect local topic associations rather than contextual embedding or learned energy dynamics. No dedicated grouped-evaluator test was identified; independent reconstruction is the verification performed here.
